Bundled fonts

Anton            — display title typeface
Cinzel           — masthead serif
Playfair Display — italic deck (PlayfairItalic.ttf)
Archivo          — kicker, facts bar, captions and interface text

Each of these four fonts is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(OFL). They are bundled locally so the app works fully offline; no font is
loaded from a network service.
